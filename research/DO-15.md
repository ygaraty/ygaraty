## Question

[Experiment 81](081-phi4-two-h100-pipeline.md) ran on H100s, because DigitalOcean had no smaller GPUs. **On the cheap 20 GB GPUs it was planned for, over a real link between two data centers, does the pipeline still give the reference's tokens, and what are its time to first token and inter-token latency?**

## Prediction

- **Tokens:** they match experiment 80's reference, as on the H100s.
- **Compute:** the RTX 4000 Ada reads memory at 360 GB/s, so each owner's 14.6 GB of weights take at least 41 ms per token. Experiment 81's kernels reached about 0.8 TB/s, so on this GPU they should be close to bandwidth-bound: about 45 ms per owner, about 90 ms of compute per token.
- **Network:** two data centers add their round trip, likely 10–100 ms, and w1's claim and post cross it about twice per token.
- **Expected inter-token latency:** 120–300 ms per token, set mostly by which 2 data centers have stock.
- **Memory:** experiment 81 peaked at 17 GB per owner (including torch's cache), so it should fit in 20 GB.

## Gate (frozen 2026-11-04, before any machine was made)

1. **Correctness:** experiment 81's gate, unchanged.
   - Use experiment 80's 3 prompts with 16 greedy tokens each.
   - Pass if each prompt's tokens equal experiment 80's float32 reference up to the first position whose reference top-2 margin is below 0.5. A divergence at or after such a position counts as a tie and is reported.
   - Every job completes, with 0 rejected results.
2. **Measurement** (reported, not pass/fail):
   - Experiment 81's protocol: 128, 1,024 and 3,968-token prompts (seed 2026), 128 new tokens, chunks of 1,024, 1 warmup plus 5 measured sessions.
   - Reported: median time to first token, median and p95 inter-token latency, tokens/s, peak GPU memory, weight-load time and span medians.
   - Also reported: the round trip between the 2 data centers.
   - Each figure is compared with experiment 81's, without a threshold.

## Protocol

- **Machines:**
  ```bash
  pods up do15 --provider runpod --server gpu:rtx4000ada --workers 1 --worker-size gpu:rtx4000ada \
    --server-data-centers <A> --worker-data-centers <B> --minutes 120 --max-hourly 1
  ```
  - Secure Cloud, $0.28/h each, on the plain `ubuntu:22.04` image with an 80 GB container disk.
  - Each pod deletes itself at its deadline.
  - Data centers are chosen from stock at go time, and the server's is never the worker's.
- **Setup:** `pods ship do15`, then `phi4-setup server` on A and `phi4-setup` on B, in parallel.
  - `phi4-setup` installs the torch build the host's driver supports (PyPI's for CUDA 13.2, otherwise cu130 or cu126).
  - Shard hashes are checked against experiment 80's.
- **Rehearsal:** the correctness job is the first job. If it fails on setup or networking, fix and redo it, within the budget.
- **Driver:** `runs/pods-data/do15/drive.py correctness`, then `drive.py timing 128 1024 3968`, then `analyze.py`.
- **Round trip:** 20 pings, measured with curl from B to A's mapped coordinator port.
- **Teardown:** `pods down do15` when the jobs finish, then `pods ls` must show 0.
- **Cost:** about $0.56/h for both. Setup (a 29 GB download on each pod) plus jobs should take about 1 h, so about $0.60. The proposed budget is $3, with deletion at 2 h.
