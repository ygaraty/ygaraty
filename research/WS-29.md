## Question

In experiment 113 a contributor stopped mid-load kept its slot for about 5 minutes: a rejoin was refused ("All contributor slots are occupied"). How long does a departed contributor hold its slot today, for a clean leave and for a crash, and can a clean leave (pagehide, process stop) free it at once and a crash within the heartbeat timeout (90 s, WS-28)?

## Prediction

A clean leave can free the slot within seconds if the page reports it; a crash waits for the heartbeat timeout plus the panel's reservation handling.

## Gate (proposed; freeze before running)

- Measure time from the stop to a successful reservation by a new contributor, 3 times per case (leave, crash).
- Target (TBD, freeze before running): clean leave ≤ 10 s, crash ≤ 120 s.

## Protocol

Local: `scripts/user_session_check.py`-style panel and coordinator, headless Chrome contributors (`invite_contributor`), a driver that stops one and polls `reserve`. Under `watch.py`. Then fix and rerun as needed.
