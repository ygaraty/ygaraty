## Question

Batching 8 conversations gave 36.2 tokens/s against 19.5 for one (experiment 110), at short context. At long context each conversation's KV grows by 16 KiB per token per stage, so under a 4 GB cap fewer conversations fit the longer they run, and admission depends on how long each has been going.

With DO-56's window and DO-57's quantized KV, each conversation's memory per stage is fixed and small.

**At 32k-token conversations under a 4 GB stage cap, how many conversations fit and what total throughput do they reach, uncapped fp16 against the best window and KV type?**

## Prediction

- **Uncapped fp16 at 32k:** 512 MiB per conversation per stage; 1 conversation fits beside the weights.
- **W = 8,192 with int8 KV:** about 64 MiB + 25 MiB per conversation per stage; 8–10 fit.
- **Throughput:** close to experiment 110's 36 tokens/s at 8 conversations, against about 12–15 for the single uncapped conversation at 32k.

## Gate

TBD, to be frozen after DO-56 and DO-57 and before the run. Proposed:
- **Counts as useful:** at 32k-token conversations under a 4.0 GB peak per stage, at least **4×** the uncapped arm's total tokens/s, with quality as DO-56 and DO-57 accepted it.
- **Report also:** conversations admitted; per-conversation time per token; peak memory per stage.

## Protocol

1. **Arms:** uncapped fp16; DO-56's window; DO-57's KV type; both.
2. **Load:** N conversations, each first filled to 32k tokens by a fixed document, then 128 batched decode tokens each; N raised until the 4.0 GB cap is reached.
3. **Hosts:** experiment 110's two RTX 3060s and coordinator; 3 repeats.
