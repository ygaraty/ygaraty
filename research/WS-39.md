## Question

ExecuTorch now runs `torch.export` models through WGSL compute shaders in the browser, with shape-routed kernels and 4-bit groupwise weights. Is it faster or slower than ours per byte of weights, kernel by kernel, and is anything in its kernels worth copying?

## Prediction

Below ours on decode GB/s: its validated scale is about 1B parameters and early; possibly better on small projections where launch overhead dominates.

## Gate (proposed; freeze before running)

Diagnostic, **Found**: decode tokens per second at short and ~1,024 positions, 3 runs each; per-kernel GPU ms and GB/s from both engines' timestamp profiles; outputs sanity-checked (different quantizations).

## Protocol

Fetch and pin the ExecuTorch WebGPU runtime and the Qwen3-0.6B export; a local page like experiment 118's WebLLM page; check the claims above against its documentation first. Repeat on the A100 in DO-67 if useful.
