## Question

When a contributor leaves mid-conversation, the session today recovers by rebuilding its state, replaying the conversation's tokens through the stages (WS-28). That costs a prefill of the whole conversation: about 24 s at 8,192 tokens (experiment 97), more for longer ones.

A stage's session state is small and structured: the DeltaNet layers' recurrent and convolution states (about 25 MiB per 16-layer stage, whatever the length) and the full-attention layers' KV (16 KiB per token per stage in fp16, or a fixed amount with DO-56's window). It can be copied to a standby contributor that already holds the stage's weights, over a direct link, while the conversation is paused.

**Planned (a contributor announces it is leaving) and unplanned (the stage keeps a running copy with a peer), how long does moving a stage's session state take against replaying the conversation, and are the following tokens identical?**

## Prediction

- **Planned move:** at 8k tokens, 25 MiB + 128 MiB over a direct link of 100–500 Mb/s: 3–12 s, against about 24 s to replay; with DO-56's window the move takes the same time at any length.
- **Unplanned:** the stage streams new KV positions and a DeltaNet snapshot every N tokens to a peer; recovery then replays only the last N tokens. Costs about 16 KiB per token of upload plus a snapshot every N tokens.
- **Tokens after the move:** identical, since the state is copied bit for bit.

## Gate

TBD, to be frozen before the rented run. Proposed:
- **Exactness (workstation, S5-nano, required):** after a planned move, the next 128 tokens are identical to an unmoved session.
- **Counts as useful:** on experiment 97's hosts, a planned move at 8k tokens takes at most **half** the replay time, tokens identical; report 32k.
- **Report also:** the unplanned variant's upload cost per token and its recovery time at N = 64, 256, 1,024.

## Protocol

1. **Code:** serialize a stage's per-session state (KV buffers, DeltaNet states, positions, the session's sampling state), send it over the session's WebRTC channel or through the relay, load it on a standby contributor, and resume; for the unplanned variant, an incremental state stream to a peer.
2. **Workstation:** S5-nano, local contributors, planned and unplanned moves at 1k and 4k tokens (`tests.test_qwen35_session_move`).
3. **Rented:** experiment 97's two stages plus one standby per stage; planned moves at 8k and 32k against replay; unplanned with N = 64, 256, 1,024; 3 repeats.
