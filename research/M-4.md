## Question

Experts in a mixture-of-experts layer are independent, and each token visits only one or a few of them. This is the way of adding owners and parameters that doesn't deepen the model; OFF-8 measures what it costs on the chain. LLaMA-MoE (EMNLP 2024) and MoEfication build MoE layers from dense FFNs; Switch Transformers train top-1 MoE from scratch.

Experiment 41 found that wider models (width 768 and 1,024) learn no faster per pair early in training. MoE adds FFN parameters without adding FLOPs per token, which is a different kind of width.

**Does a 4-expert top-1 FFN reach a lower held-out loss than the dense FFN at 32,768 pairs?**

## Prediction

- **Loss:** the 4×2048 MoE gains under 0.05 at 32,768 pairs. The model is small and the training early, and experiment 41 points the same way. This is likely to fail the gate, and a fail is worth knowing before any distributed work.
- **The equal-parameter arm** (4 experts of 512) loses 0.02–0.1 against dense at a quarter of the FFN FLOPs.
- **Load balance:** with an auxiliary balancing loss of 0.01, the busiest expert takes at most 1.5× the mean load.

## Gate

TBD, to be frozen before any run. Proposed:
- **Primary:** E4×2048 at least **0.05** lower mean held-out loss than dense over 2 seeds at 32,768 pairs.
- **Required alongside:** the busiest expert's share of tokens at most 1.5× the mean, averaged over the last 4,096 pairs. If not, report the result as unbalanced.
- **Report also:** E4×512 against dense, and the dropped-token fraction at capacity factor 1.25.

## Protocol

1. **Code:** `--moe-experts E --moe-hidden h --moe-top-k 1` in `batch_learning`.
   - Switch-style router (a linear layer to E logits, softmax, top-1).
   - The expert output is scaled by its router probability.
   - Capacity factor 1.25: tokens over capacity skip the FFN through the residual.
   - Auxiliary load-balancing loss with coefficient 0.01.
   - Applies to every FFN in the encoder and decoder.
2. **Arms:**

   | Arm | FFN | FFN parameters | FFN FLOPs per token |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | D | dense 2048 (experiment 67's Adam arm, WS-3; reuse it if on the same commit) | 1× | 1× |
   | E4×2048 | 4 experts of 2048, top-1 | 4× | 1× |
   | E4×512 | 4 experts of 512, top-1 | 1× | ¼× |

3. **Runs:** 2 seeds (2026, 2027), 32,768 pairs, held-out loss every 4,096 pairs. Log the expert loads every 512 pairs.
4. **Prepare on the workstation:** a unit test that E = 1 with top-1 and a router fixed at probability 1 reproduces the dense FFN exactly.

## Trigger on the workstation

```bash
git pull && pip install -e . && python -m unittest tests.test_moe_ffn
export YGARATY_DATASET_URL=sqlite:///$PWD/runs/datasets/opus100-en-pt/opus100-en-pt.sqlite
COMMON="--embedding 512 --hidden 2048 --heads 8 --layers 3 --version 1m-seed2026 --batch 64 --length-window 64 --batch-tokens 3072 --pairs 32768 --eval-every 4096 --optimizer adam --learning-rate 0.0005"
for seed in 2026 2027; do
  ygaraty experiment batch_learning $COMMON --seed $seed --output runs/experiments/M-4/D-$seed
  ygaraty experiment batch_learning $COMMON --seed $seed --moe-experts 4 --moe-hidden 2048 --moe-top-k 1 --output runs/experiments/M-4/E4x2048-$seed
  ygaraty experiment batch_learning $COMMON --seed $seed --moe-experts 4 --moe-hidden 512 --moe-top-k 1 --output runs/experiments/M-4/E4x512-$seed
done
```

The `--moe-*` flags are what has to be built. Flags in `COMMON` follow WS-3's trigger; check them against the current `batch_learning --help`.
