## Question

WebLLM reads 4.47 GB per Qwen3.5-9B token against our 5.10 GB (experiment 118): Q4_K_M keeps some tensors at 5 and 6 bits. A uniform 4-bit groupwise layout reads fewer bytes and needs simpler decoding. How much faster, and how much accuracy is lost by requantizing?

## Prediction

About 12% fewer bytes per token and 10–12% faster decode on the 680M (bandwidth-bound since experiment 121); greedy tokens mostly unchanged but not all (requantization).

## Gate (proposed; freeze before running)

Correctness against the NumPy reference of the **original** GGUF: tokens equal over 32 steps except where the reference's top-2 margin is small, logits within a tolerance to set before running (the K-quant gate's 2e-3 likely too tight for requantized weights); plus a perplexity check on a fixed text. **Worth it** if decode is at least 8% faster with the agreed accuracy.

## Protocol

Repack in the stage loaders (one-time cost measured); kernel in `webgpu_kernels.mts`; feature-flagged.
