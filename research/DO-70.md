## Question

Experiment 123: WebGPU in Chrome copies at 725 GB/s on an A100 SXM4 (2,039 GB/s HBM), which caps one conversation near 128 tokens per second. Is the limit Chrome, Dawn's Vulkan backend, the container's driver, or our probe?

## Prediction

CUDA copy 1.5–1.8 TB/s; native Dawn close to Chrome (the limit is in Dawn or Vulkan, not the page); a read kernel with wide loads above the buffer copy.

## Gate (proposed; freeze before running)

Diagnostic, **Found**: GB/s per path, median of 5; "browser limit" if native Dawn exceeds Chrome by more than 25%.
