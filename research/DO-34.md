## Question

[Experiment 84](084-persistent-phi4-sessions.md) halved the time between tokens on emulated links with a tiny model. **With real GPU compute (an A16 reads 8.8 GB of bf16 weights per owner per token) on a real LAN, what do persistent sessions save, with the same tokens?**

## Prediction

- Compute dominates: an A16 has about 200 GB/s, so about 45 ms per owner per token, about 90–100 ms per token in all.
- On a sub-millisecond VPC, the step path's coordination costs a few ms per hop plus Python, so persistent sessions should save 5–20%.

## Gate (frozen 2026-11-05, before any machine was made; the user's yes)

1. **Correctness:** every job completes with 0 rejected results, and the greedy tokens are identical across arms (experiment 83's 3 chat prompts, 16 tokens each, and every timing session).
2. **Measurement** (reported, not pass/fail): jobs in the order A B B A at 128 and 1,024-token prompts (seed 2026), each 1 warmup plus 5 measured sessions of 128 new tokens. Reported: median and p95 inter-token latency and their reduction, time to first token, per-owner spans.

## Protocol

- **Machines:** `runs/pods-data/vultr_drive.py do34 up`, through `phi4_cluster.Cluster` (setup "2x A16 16 GB, bf16"), watchdog at 60 min.
- **Jobs:** `vultr_drive.py do34 correctness`, then `timing`; then `down`, and `pods ls` must show 0.
- **Cost:** $0.471/h each, $0.94/h for both. Budget: $0.94, deleted at 60 min.
