## Question

Commit `13d74e6` added 4-bit weights as an option. It cuts Phi-4 from 29.3 GB to about 9.3 GB, enough for one 12 GB GPU ($0.13–0.18/h), but its effect on real tokens is unknown. **How far do plain round-to-nearest 4-bit weights move Phi-4's next-token choices from experiment 80's float32 reference?**

## Prediction

- **Matrices:** each layer's matrices have a relative error of about 0.10; layer 0's qkv has 0.13.
- **Tokens:** published round-to-nearest 4-bit results at groups of 128 usually cost a few percent of perplexity on 7–14B models. So I expect top-1 agreement of about 90–97% when fed the reference's tokens, and free greedy tokens that diverge after the first near-tie.
- **Text:** the France answer should still read "The capital of France is Paris."

## Gate (proposed; needs a yes before running)

- **Inputs:** experiment 80's 3 prompts (19, 95 and 128 tokens) and its 16 reference tokens each, so 48 teacher-forced positions. Reference logits come from experiment 80's `logits-{p}.npz`.
- **Pass (int4 in bf16, the GPU arm):**
  1. At the positions whose reference top-2 margin is at least 0.5, the int4 argmax equals the reference's at 90% or more.
  2. Prompt 0's free greedy continuation decodes to text starting with "The capital of France is Paris".
- **Reported:**
  - free greedy tokens up to the first divergence, and the reference margin there;
  - the maximum and median logit difference;
  - the KL divergence from the reference at each position;
  - the same figures for int4 in float32 and for experiment 80's bf16;
  - per-layer matrix errors.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Pass: **Works**; int4 can be used for a cheap-GPU trial.
  - Fail: **Rejected**, for plain round to nearest. A calibrated format (GPTQ or AWQ) would be its own experiment.

## Protocol

- **Driver:** extend `ygaraty/inference/phi4_layerwise.py` with int4 sessions. Each layer is loaded once, quantized and dequantized, then run for each session, as experiment 80 did for bf16. The teacher-forced pass uses the reference tokens.
- **Run:**
  ```bash
  python -m ygaraty.inference.phi4_layerwise runs/models/phi-4 --int4 --out runs/experiments/WS-11/<host>-<timestamp>
  ```
  - Under `watch.py` (`--stall 300`, progress per layer).
  - About 30 minutes on the workstation's 16 threads, by experiment 80's time plus the int4 sessions.
- **Cost:** free; nothing remote.
